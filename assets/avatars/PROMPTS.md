The football symbols were generated separately with the built-in image-generation workflow. Existing artwork under `assets/images/` was supplied as style reference only.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square mobile game avatar master
Input images: the supplied Pundit images are style references only; match their angular faceted geometric illustration language, simplified silhouette, warm nostalgic shading and restrained detail. Do not copy their people or compositions.
Style/medium: polished flat raster illustration that looks vector-authored; angular polygon facets; no outlines; minimal detail; bold silhouette.
Composition/framing: square master; one subject occupies about 52% of the canvas; generous even padding; fully inside the circle.
Color palette: darkest #2F2926 with Pundit green #34855B, terracotta #D07158, warm cream and tan. Never use #ff00ff in the circle or subject.
Lighting/mood: restrained directional faceted shading, nostalgic and confident; no cast shadow.
Scene/backdrop: a perfectly circular muted pastel background centered on a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff outer square for later background removal. Leave a clear magenta margin on all four sides. The #ff00ff appears only outside the circle and is perfectly uniform.
Constraints: one isolated, generic football symbol; no written text, letters or numbers; no logo; no club, sponsor, manufacturer, broadcaster, transport-company, national-team or competition marks; no watermark; no border or thick outline; no photorealism; crisp edges; readable at 24 pixels.
```
